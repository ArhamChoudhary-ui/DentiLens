# AI Disclosure

## DentiLens XR — Samsung PRISM XR Hackathon 2026

**Team Name:** Rajasthani
**Institution:** Vellore Institute of Technology
**Track:** Track 4 — Action-Aware Egocentric Video Understanding and Intelligent Summarization

### 1. Project Development and Team Contribution

DentiLens XR was developed by Team Rajasthani as an AI-powered system for understanding first-person videos and generating structured, time-aware summaries.

The team was responsible for the project direction, problem-solving, technical decisions, system architecture, integration of components, implementation choices, testing, debugging, and preparation of the final submission.

The team independently evaluated implementation approaches, worked on system functionality, and reviewed the outputs produced by the application.

### 2. Use of AI Tools

AI tools were used as supporting development assistants during selected stages of the project.

Their assistance included:

- Helping troubleshoot programming errors and suggest debugging approaches.
- Providing suggestions for code improvements and alternatives.
- Assisting with explanations of technical concepts and libraries.
- Supporting documentation, README preparation and presentation refinement.
- Helping review and improve selected parts of the development workflow.

AI assistance was used as a productivity aid. Suggestions and generated material were reviewed, adapted, integrated and tested by the team rather than being accepted automatically.

### 3. Technical Implementation

The team worked on integrating the video inference pipeline, activity classification, temporal action outputs, highlight extraction, JSON generation and local validation.

The final system was tested through local execution and automated tests. The team remains responsible for the submitted implementation and its reported results.

### 4. Data and Evaluation Integrity

The system generates predictions through its implemented inference pipeline. Ground-truth annotations were not intentionally copied into prediction outputs, and missing benchmark results were not fabricated.

At the time of this release, 8 of the 187 videos listed in the organizer's manifest had been processed. The remaining 179 source videos were unavailable to the team at that checkpoint. These results are disclosed as partial benchmark coverage, not a completed official evaluation.

### 5. Declaration

Team Rajasthani acknowledges the use of AI-assisted development tools while affirming its responsibility for the project's technical direction, engineering decisions, implementation integration, testing, verification and final submission.

The team has made reasonable efforts to review the submitted work and ensure that the reported capabilities and limitations accurately reflect the current implementation.
