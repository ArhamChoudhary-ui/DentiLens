# EgoSight XR AI disclosure

Team Rajasthani used Codex to assist with implementation, debugging, tests,
documentation, a generated concept illustration and submission preparation. The team supplied the project
direction and organizer materials and is responsible for reviewing and
submitting the entry.

Track 4 predictions come from the checked-in inference code and documented
pretrained models. The release reports distinguish local validation,
benchmark limitations and pending organizer checks.

Codex also assisted with documenting the two capabilities and building and verifying the
video review interface and the connection to the existing inference engine. Dental suggestions use separate models from the
Track 4 benchmark. FPVSum reference annotations were used for earlier
diagnostics only and are excluded from training and tuning.

The EgoSight XR integration uses the existing pretrained checkpoints. No
additional training or tuning was performed for this release.
