# Team Rajasthani submission

DentiLens XR has two capabilities. Dental Intelligence is the original dental
review workspace. The independent Action Intelligence Engine is the Samsung
XR Hackathon Track 4 entry.

The archive contains **101 of 185 eligible outputs**. All 101 passed the
local checker. The original manifest has 187 IDs. Samsung excluded two
unavailable FPVSum videos, and 84 eligible outputs remain. Samsung confirmed
teams can use their own syntax and edge-case checker. Official scores are
pending. The archive uses the local development identifier while the Samsung
TEAM_ID remains unverified. Checkpoint retrieval instructions and hashes are
included.
