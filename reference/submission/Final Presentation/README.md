## Assets and evidence

- `Cover.jpg` is an ImageGen concept illustration. The prompt requested a vertical editorial still from a first-person chest- or head-mounted viewpoint, with a hand turning a kitchen timer, dark slate and stainless steel, restrained cyan daylight, and no text, faces, logos or overlays. It depicts a concept scene.
- `Demo Input.png`, `Demo Inference.png`, `Demo Timeline.png`, and `Demo Output.png` are frames from the 48-second public walkthrough at 9, 18, 26, and 37 seconds. The walkthrough displays actual model data and a timeline.
- `Benchmark Snapshot.json` records local validation for 54 manifest predictions. Official validation and scores are pending.
- Organizer requirements come from *XR Hackathon 2026 Consolidated Slides*, pages 19–22 and 25, and the Track 4 student guide.
